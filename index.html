Linette’s approach to leads

Every inquiry is captured to build the email marketing list and gets the Next Steps reply from Gmail. A contact record is created in Tern only once the lead is qualified, and a full trip record only after the consultation confirms they are ready to move forward.

ACT III

The Execution

“Throughout your journey, I am there.”

06

Booked | Post-Booking

Gets a booking confirmation, then payment reminders as balances come due.

Linette

  • Submit commission notes

  • Pay suppliers when authorized
  • Apply workflow 05 so status moves to Traveling and Traveled on trip dates

Automated emails

  • Inactive You’re booked

Forms

Pre-Departure | Final Prep
