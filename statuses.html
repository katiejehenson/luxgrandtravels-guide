Trip statuses

The status on a trip tells Linette where the client is and drives which workflows and automations run. Numbered statuses follow the journey in order; the last three are housekeeping.

CodeStatusWorkflowNotes
Consultation
01Lead / InquiryA qualified inquiry becomes a trip in Tern. The client has submitted the trip form or been pre-qualified.01. Inbound Lead – Tern Trip Form SubmitContacts are only created once a lead is qualified; full trip records follow the consultation.
02Schedule & Intake CallThe consultation is booked and held. Linette learns how the client likes to travel.—No workflow attached. The recap email (agreement + planning fee) is sent manually after the call.
Curation
03Quote Planning (Fee pd / Agmt signed)Agreement signed and planning fee paid. Supplier outreach and proposal building begin.02. All Trips: Quote PlanningTriggers the “Agreement Signed & Fee Received” and “Travel Insurance” automations.
04Quote Sent | Awaiting ApprovalThe proposal is with the client. Follow-ups and revisions happen here.02. All Trips: Quote Planning“Quote Sent” automation is built but inactive.
05Quote Approved | Pending Deposit & DocumentsThe client has said yes. Deposit authorization and traveler profile details are collected.02. All Trips: Quote Planning“Proposal Approved” automation sends the deposit + client profile email.
Execution
06Booked | Post-BookingDeposit paid and confirmed with suppliers. Extra elements may still be added.03. All Trips: Payments
04. Booked | Pre/ Post Travel		Both the Payments and Pre/Post Travel workflows are set to apply here.
06BPre-Departure | Final PrepFinal paperwork, itinerary review and pre-trip touchpoints.04. Booked | Pre/ Post TravelDate-based automations from workflow 04 keep running.
07TravelingThe client is on the trip. Arrival check-in and on-trip support.—
Afterward
08TraveledHome again. Welcome home survey and future-travel follow-up.—Leave trips here until commission is collected.
Closed
—CancelledTrip did not go ahead.—Hidden from view.
—ArchivedCompleted trip, commission collected.—Hidden from view. Workflow 04 creates an “Archive Trip” task 181 days after the trip ends.
—Landing Page DuplicatesHolding status for duplicate trips created by landing-page form submissions.—Purpose inferred from the name; confirm with Linette.
Heads up

Auto-apply is currently switched off on every workflow, so changing a status does not attach a workflow on its own yet. Workflows are applied by hand or with Bulk Apply. See To Review.
